Small pill label for product flags and status tags.

```jsx
<Badge tone="bestseller">Best Seller</Badge>
<Badge tone="popular">Popular</Badge>
<Badge tone="new">New</Badge>
<Badge tone="sale">-40%</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral`, `primary`, `bestseller` (amber), `popular` (indigo), `new` (emerald), `success`, `sale` (rose). Sizes `sm`/`md`.
