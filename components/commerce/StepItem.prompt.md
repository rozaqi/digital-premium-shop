One numbered step in the "Cara Order" stepper (4 steps, laid out in a row/grid).

```jsx
<StepItem number={1} title="Pilih produk" description="Cari akun premium yang kamu mau." />
<StepItem number={4} title="Akun dikirim" description="Akun siap dipakai." last />
```

Use `last` on the final step to drop the connector line. Put 4 in a responsive grid.
